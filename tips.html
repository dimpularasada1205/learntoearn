Top Tips & Tricks to Crack GATE

1. Understand the Syllabus Completely

Start by thoroughly analyzing the GATE syllabus. Focus on important topics and weightage distribution before beginning preparation.

2. Make a Realistic Study Plan

Create a proper timetable covering all subjects. Allocate more time to difficult subjects and revise regularly.

3. Strengthen Fundamentals

Build strong basics in core subjects like Mathematics and your branch-specific subjects. Concept clarity is key to scoring high.

4. Practice Previous Year Questions

Solving previous year GATE papers helps you understand exam pattern, difficulty level, and important topics.

5. Take Regular Mock Tests

Attempt mock tests weekly to improve speed, accuracy, and time management skills.

6. Revise Frequently

Revision is very important. Maintain short notes and revise formulas and key concepts regularly.

7. Stay Consistent & Positive

Consistency matters more than studying long hours. Stay motivated and maintain a positive mindset.
