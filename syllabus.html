GATE Exam Syllabus

The GATE syllabus includes General Aptitude and core engineering subjects. Below is a general overview of important topics commonly covered in the exam.

General Aptitude

  • Verbal Ability
  • Numerical Ability
  • Analytical Reasoning
  • Quantitative Aptitude

Engineering Mathematics

  • Linear Algebra
  • Calculus
  • Differential Equations
  • Probability & Statistics

Core Subjects

  • Data Structures
  • Operating Systems
  • Computer Networks
  • Database Management Systems

Technical Topics

  • Digital Logic
  • Computer Organization
  • Software Engineering
  • Theory of Computation
